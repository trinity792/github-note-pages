# Week 11 - Applied Ethics
## L27 Environmental Contexts and Consequences of Data
### Takeaways
- All data (and data technologies) are material 
	- Resources like land, minerals, water, and energy are critical inputs
	- Vs. familiar socio-technical stories (“clean tech,” “the cloud”)
- Actors near and far are involved, even if they’re not visible
	- Geography (physical distance, geographical features, human settlement)
	- Planetary networks, flows of materials and labor
	- Many of these features are made invisible
	- And costs are pushed out into externalities
- Environmental justice begins by undoing invisibilization and externalization
	- And goes beyond that: recognizing dynamics and structures of power
	- And then asks: What can you do? Where can you start?
