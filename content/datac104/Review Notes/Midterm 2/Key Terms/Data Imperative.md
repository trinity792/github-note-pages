# Data Imperative
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 9/Mona Sloane - The Business of AI|Sloane: the business of AI]].

## Definition and Significance
The data imperative is the drive to generate and accumulate information because it is expected to yield future value. L24 describes data collection becoming a central business strategy even before firms know exactly how to monetize their records.

The course links this imperative to investment expectations and the prediction paradigm. Sloane questions treating data as a naturally available resource, emphasizing the social activity, labor, and institutional arrangements that produce it.

## Examples

- A social platform, tractor, robot vacuum, or grocery store can all be organized as sources of behavioral information.
