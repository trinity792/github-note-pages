# Palantir
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 9/Tianyu M Fang - Silicon Valley Goes To War|Fang: Silicon Valley goes to war]].

## Definition and Significance
Palantir is the data-organization and analytics company discussed through its work for governments, militaries, and businesses. The course connects its emergence to Peter Thiel, private financing, and early intelligence-linked investment.

The company exemplifies the fusion of platform businesses with state functions. Rhetoric about disrupting bureaucracy accompanies dependence on public contracts and national-security priorities, raising questions about influence and accountability.
