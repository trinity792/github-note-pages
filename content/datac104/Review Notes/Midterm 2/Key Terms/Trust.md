# Trust
Lectures: L14. Sources: [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 6/Theodore Porter's Objectivity and Authority - How French Engineers Reduced Public Utility to Numbers|Porter: objectivity and authority]]; [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]].

## Definition and Significance
Trust concerns the credibility people grant to institutions, experts, or knowledge claims. L14 examines how impersonal calculations can substitute for personal familiarity when decisions must persuade distant or skeptical audiences.

Porter's point is historical and political: quantified procedures often become attractive when authority cannot simply be taken for granted. Numerical presentation can secure confidence without eliminating the judgments underlying the measure.

- French engineers used public-utility formulas to defend decisions that outsiders might otherwise attribute to favoritism.
