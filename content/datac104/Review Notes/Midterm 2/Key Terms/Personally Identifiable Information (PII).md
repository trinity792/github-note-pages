# Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
PII is information that identifies, distinguishes, or can be linked to an individual. The notes include direct identifiers and information associated with them, making identifiability a major organizing concept in privacy governance.

L12 argues that privacy concerns extend beyond identifying a named person. Changed purposes, group interests, bodily dignity, and institutional power can matter even when shared information has been stripped of direct identifiers.
