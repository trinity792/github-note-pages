# Discipline
Lectures: L17. Sources: [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 6/Michel Foucault's Discipline and Punish (Panopticism)|Foucault: panopticism]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]].

## Definition and Significance
Discipline is power that organizes, observes, compares, and trains people so their conduct conforms to institutional expectations. In Foucault's account, individuals can internalize surveillance and regulate themselves.

The prediction lectures connect this mechanism to data-based classification. People learn to anticipate how institutions will score them, so power works through self-management as well as direct commands or punishment.

---

## Examples

- The Panopticon's uncertain observer encourages continuous self-monitoring.
- Managing a credit score shows conduct shaped by anticipated future evaluation.
