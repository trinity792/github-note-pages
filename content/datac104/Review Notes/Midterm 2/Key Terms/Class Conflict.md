# Class Conflict
Lectures: L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L20 AI Futures|L20: AI futures]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 8/David F. Noble - Social Choice in Machine Design The Case of Automatically Controlled Machine Tools|Noble: social choice in machine design]].

## Definition and Significance
Class conflict concerns struggles over resources, production, control, and the distribution of life chances. L20 reads some AI and longevity stories as ways of imagining conflicts between those who command labor and those who perform it.

The lecture links fear of machine rebellion to hierarchies involving workers, enslaved people, and colonial subjects. Positionality matters: losing control appears differently to those who already possess considerable power than to those with little.

---

## Examples

- Blade Runner's replicants perform dangerous labor under deliberately limited lifespans.
- Noble's machinists and managers contest who controls production knowledge and pacing.
