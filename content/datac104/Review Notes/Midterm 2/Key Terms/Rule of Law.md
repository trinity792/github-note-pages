# Rule of Law
Lectures: L14. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 8/Hildebrandt – Introduction-Dianas OnLife World|Hildebrandt: Diana's onlife world]].

## Definition and Significance
The rule of law constrains arbitrary authority through public rules and opportunities for legal contestation. Hildebrandt emphasizes democratic enactment, the possibility of disobedience, and independent judicial challenge as features of modern democratic law.

Quantified administration can support predictable procedures, but technical enforcement is not automatically equivalent to lawful governance. Privately designed systems can remove choices or obscure challenges before a person can invoke rights.
