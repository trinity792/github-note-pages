# Proxies
Lectures: L16. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L16 Fairness Automated Decision-Making I|L16: fairness]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 7/Virginia Eubanks' Automating Inequality (Chapter 4)|Eubanks: the Allegheny algorithm]].

## Definition and Significance
A proxy is a measurable variable used to stand in for a phenomenon that is difficult to observe directly. Its suitability depends on how it relates to the intended target and how institutions produce it.

The course emphasizes that improving prediction of a proxy is not identical to improving understanding of the underlying phenomenon. Models can reproduce the decisions and biases embedded in substitute measures.

---

## Examples

- Arrest is a proxy for crime but also records policing.
- AFST predicts re-referral and foster placement as substitutes for direct future child harm.
