# Neoliberalism
Lectures: L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Neoliberalism is the late twentieth-century political orientation described in L18 through privatization, deregulation, financialization, weakened labor, and expanding market logic. It increasingly addresses people as consumers responsible for managing their own risks.

The lecture describes a co-produced relationship with automated assessment, rather than a simple technological cause. Systems testing deservingness and pricing personal risk fit and reinforce this conception of responsible governance.
