# Privacy Policy
Lectures: L12. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
A privacy policy states an organization's practices and commitments concerning information it collects and uses. Within the model discussed in L12, these promises help define what users accept and what regulators may seek to enforce.

The course treats reading and managing policies as labor assigned to individuals. Disclosure alone does not remove unequal bargaining power, make the document comprehensible, or ensure that every announced use fits the original relationship.
