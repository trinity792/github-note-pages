# Social Security
Lectures: L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Social Security is the lecture's example of social insurance intended to provide shared protection through collective contributions. Its solidaristic principle treats security as a social entitlement rather than a price calculated separately for each person's predicted risk.

L18 distinguishes that ideal from its imperfect historical realization. The example asks whether protection follows citizenship and need or whether each recipient must establish individual deservingness.

## Examples

- Risks such as widespread unemployment or economic depression are collective conditions that individuals cannot simply choose to avoid.
