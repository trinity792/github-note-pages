# Behaviorism
Lectures: L15. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]].

## Definition and Significance
Behaviorism models behavior through relationships among stimuli, actions, and consequences. L15 emphasizes Skinner's operant conditioning, where reinforcement changes the likelihood of subsequent actions.

The course treats designed environments as active producers of behavior rather than neutral places of observation. These mechanisms connect experimental training to gamification, reinforcement learning, and ambitions to organize society through behavioral intervention.

---

## Examples

- Skinner boxes use rewards or punishments to establish repeatable action patterns.
- Skinner's fictional Walden Two extends behavioral design to an imagined social order.
