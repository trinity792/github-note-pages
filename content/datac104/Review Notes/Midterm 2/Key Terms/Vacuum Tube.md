# Vacuum Tube
Lectures: L22. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
A vacuum tube is the electronic device L22 describes using a heated cathode, an anode, and a controlling grid inside an evacuated enclosure. Its arrangement allows a weak input to control a stronger output.

Vacuum tubes enabled generating, detecting, and amplifying signals for wireless communications and early electronics. The lecture connects their technical function to amateur communities, manufacturers, and military communications needs.

---

## Examples

- Ship-to-shore communication required wireless transmission because a moving ship could not remain attached to a telegraph wire.
