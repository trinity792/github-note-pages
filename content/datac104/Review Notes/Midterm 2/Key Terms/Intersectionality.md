# Intersectionality
Lectures: L16. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L16 Fairness Automated Decision-Making I|L16: fairness]].

## Definition and Significance
Intersectionality examines social positions and forms of oppression that cannot be reduced to independent identity attributes. L16 situates the concept in Black feminist thought and cautions against treating identities as an additive tally.

The concept matters for auditing because single-axis comparisons can conceal distinctive patterns affecting intersecting groups. It also requires attention to historical and institutional relationships beyond merely creating more statistical categories.

## Examples

- Gender Shades found especially poor performance for darker-skinned female faces, a disparity obscured by looking only at gender or skin tone separately.
