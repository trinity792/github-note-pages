# Welfare State
Lectures: L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
The welfare state institutionalizes collective responsibility for social risks and material security. L18 connects its development to industrial accidents, organized labor, and recognition that unemployment or injury cannot always be understood as personal failures.

The lecture contrasts this solidaristic ideal with governance centered on individual deservingness and risk pricing. It also notes that universal aspirations can coexist with racial exclusions and unequal implementation.

---

## Examples

- The New Deal and Social Security exemplify the course's U.S. history of collective protection.
