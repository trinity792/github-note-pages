# Recidivism
Lectures: L16. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L16 Fairness Automated Decision-Making I|L16: fairness]].

## Definition and Significance
Recidivism refers to returning to offending after earlier involvement with criminal justice. The course examines it as the target of risk-assessment systems, while asking how later conduct becomes recorded and classified.

Prediction depends on an operational definition: arrest, conviction, and imprisonment are not interchangeable observations. Measures can encode policing and institutional decisions as well as people's actions, complicating claims to neutral ground truth.
