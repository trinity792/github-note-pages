# Terms of Service
Lectures: L12. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
Terms of service are the agreements defining the relationship between a user and a service provider. L12 describes acceptance at signup as a central mechanism through which companies obtain authorization for practices involving users' information.

The lecture questions how much practical influence acceptance provides. Agreements can remain consequential after ownership, preferences, or the user's relationship with the service changes, while refusing them may mean losing access altogether.
