# Transistor
Lectures: L22. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
A transistor is the solid-state electronic device developed at Bell Labs in 1947 and discussed as an alternative to the vacuum tube. The lecture describes semiconductor materials enabling signal amplification and detection.

Its regional significance depends on institutions and mobile technical labor. Shockley's move from Bell Labs helped establish a semiconductor enterprise whose employees later contributed to other Silicon Valley firms.
