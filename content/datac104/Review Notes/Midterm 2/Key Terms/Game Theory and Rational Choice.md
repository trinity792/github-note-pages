# Game Theory and Rational Choice
Lectures: L15. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]].

## Definition and Significance
Game theory formalizes strategic choice, while rational choice models agents selecting actions that maximize payoff or expected utility. L15 presents them as approaches making optimal decisions describable through mathematical rules.

The lecture distinguishes a normative model of rational action from a complete description of actual behavior. Making good choice calculable also makes it possible to imagine machines implementing the same decision procedure.

---

## Examples

- Cold War nuclear strategy made avoiding disastrous choices a powerful justification for formal decision models.
