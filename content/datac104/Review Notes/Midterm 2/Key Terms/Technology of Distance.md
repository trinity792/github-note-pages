# Technology of Distance
Lectures: L14. Sources: [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 6/Theodore Porter's Objectivity and Authority - How French Engineers Reduced Public Utility to Numbers|Porter: objectivity and authority]]; [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]].

## Definition and Significance
A technology of distance enables knowledge and decisions to travel among people who lack close personal relationships. In the course's account of Porter, standardized quantification supports communication and coordination across institutions and a society of strangers.

Numbers can appear credible because their procedures are portable and impersonal. That communicative strength also warrants scrutiny of the categories and assumptions that become authoritative when local context is compressed.

- A public-utility calculation lets officials compare infrastructure projects without personally sharing every engineer's practical experience.
