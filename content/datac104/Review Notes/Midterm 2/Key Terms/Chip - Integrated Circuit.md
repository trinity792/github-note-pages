# Chip - Integrated Circuit
Lectures: L22. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
An integrated circuit, or chip, places multiple electronic components on a semiconductor wafer. L22 emphasizes early chips' small size, speed, and durability alongside their difficult, costly, and failure-prone manufacture.

Military procurement supported production before broad consumer markets emerged. The case challenges an account in which commercial superiority alone explains technological success: early customers financed learning and improvements in manufacturing.

## Examples

- Aircraft and missiles supplied demanding environments in which compact, robust electronics were valuable.
