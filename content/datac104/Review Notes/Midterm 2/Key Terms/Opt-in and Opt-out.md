# Opt-in and Opt-out
Lectures: L12. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]].

## Definition and Significance
Opt-in requires an affirmative action to enter a data practice; opt-out requires action to leave or refuse a practice that otherwise applies. The distinction concerns the default from which a person's choice begins.

The course connects defaults to choice architecture. A formally available refusal can still impose effort or loss of service, while default participation favors collection. Evaluating consent therefore requires examining the practical conditions of choice.
