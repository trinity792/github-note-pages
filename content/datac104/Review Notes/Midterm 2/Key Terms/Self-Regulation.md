# Self-Regulation
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
Self-regulation refers here to organizations setting and administering their own data-practice commitments, often through published policies and user agreements. Public enforcement may then focus on whether organizations honor those commitments.

The course questions whether this model meaningfully restrains collection when companies also define the terms of participation. The ability to announce a practice and obtain formal acceptance does not establish that users can negotiate it or avoid the surrounding data economy.
