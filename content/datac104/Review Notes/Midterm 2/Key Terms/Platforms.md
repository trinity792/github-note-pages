# Platforms
Lectures: L23. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L23 Making Silicon Valley II|L23: Silicon Valley II]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
Platforms are company-organized digital infrastructures facilitating interactions among different groups, such as buyers and sellers. L24 describes them as multi-sided markets whose owners can extract value from transactions, advertising, or data.

Platforms govern the conditions of participation rather than merely connecting users neutrally. Network effects and venture-funded expansion can produce dependence and make market dominance a source of institutional power.

---

## Examples

- Uber organizes passenger-driver interactions and sets terms for both sides.
- Social platforms become more attractive as users find more of their contacts there.
