# Ohlone
Lectures: L22. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
The Ohlone are the Indigenous people whose presence L22 identifies before the region's colonization and its later agricultural and technological development. Their inclusion changes where Silicon Valley's history begins.

The lecture uses this history to expose narrative omission. Starting with white settlement and orchards can erase Indigenous presence, colonization, and violence, making later development seem to arise on an uncomplicated landscape.
