# Automation
Lectures: L21. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L21 Automation and Labor|L21: automation and labor]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 8/David F. Noble - Social Choice in Machine Design The Case of Automatically Controlled Machine Tools|Noble: social choice in machine design]].

## Definition and Significance
Automation delegates activities or steps of production to technical systems. L21 distinguishes augmentation, which enhances human activity, from substitution, which takes over particular activities, while emphasizing that the boundary is contested.

The course treats automation as a sociotechnical process embedded in ownership, labor organization, and power. A system can change who controls knowledge and pace while still depending on substantial human work.
