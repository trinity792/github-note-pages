# Ranking
Lectures: L17. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Ranking arranges people or institutions in an ordered hierarchy according to selected measures. It converts differences into positions that can guide access to opportunities and resources.

The lectures treat this ordering as performative. Scores help produce unequal futures and encourage people to manage the representations on which institutions judge them, rather than merely describing pre-existing differences.

---

## Examples

- School grades channel students into later opportunities.
- Credit scores affect access to financing and the price of borrowing.
