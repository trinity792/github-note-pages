# Cognitive Science
Lectures: L15. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]].

## Definition and Significance
Cognitive science appears in L15 as an approach modeling thought through information processing. People use simplifications, schemas, and heuristics because the world exceeds their capacity for comprehensive calculation.

Herbert Simon's bounded rationality links this account to organizational decision-making. Decisions are constructed under limits of time, knowledge, and complexity; rendering problems manageable also shapes which alternatives become available.

---

## Examples

- Simon treats bureaucracies as information-processing systems that organize problems into decidable form.
