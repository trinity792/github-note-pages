# De-identification and Re-identification
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
De-identification removes or obscures identifying details from a dataset; re-identification links apparently anonymous records back to people, often by combining them with other data. The course emphasizes that removing names does not necessarily prevent identification.

The significance is relational: a dataset's privacy risk depends on other available records and the ability to link them. Anonymity cannot be assessed solely by inspecting whether one isolated file contains obvious identifiers.

## Examples

- Sweeney's linkage of health-insurance and voter records illustrates identification through combined demographic attributes.
- The Netflix ratings example shows how public IMDb reviews could help connect anonymized viewing records to users.
