# Nvidia
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
Nvidia is the chip producer used in L24 to illustrate the hardware layer of the AI boom. Its graphics processors, initially associated with video games, became important resources for AI training and inference.

The course emphasizes infrastructure as a route to capturing value. A hardware supplier can benefit from demand for essential components even while firms buying those components search for profitable applications.
