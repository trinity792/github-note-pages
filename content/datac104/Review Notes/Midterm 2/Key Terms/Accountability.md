# Accountability
Lectures: L14. Sources: [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 6/Theodore Porter's Objectivity and Authority - How French Engineers Reduced Public Utility to Numbers|Porter: objectivity and authority]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]].

## Definition and Significance
Accountability concerns the ability to explain decisions, identify responsibility, and subject institutional action to scrutiny. In the quantification discussion, rule-bound calculations provide a defensible record of how a decision was reached.

The prediction lectures show why a traceable procedure alone may be insufficient. People also need meaningful ways to understand and contest consequential classifications, including decisions implemented through opaque technical systems.
