# Luddites
Lectures: L21. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L21 Automation and Labor|L21: automation and labor]].

## Definition and Significance
The Luddites were skilled textile workers whose machine-breaking opposed technological deployments threatening livelihoods and working conditions. L21 presents their actions within struggles over wages, craft autonomy, and factory discipline.

The example challenges the use of Luddite as a synonym for irrational hostility to technology. The political question concerns which machinery is introduced, under whose control, and with what consequences for workers.

---

## Examples

- Machine-breaking targeted the organization of production and its effects on labor, rather than demonstrating that workers rejected every technical innovation.
