# PATRIOT Act (2001)
Lectures: L13. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
The PATRIOT Act is the post-September 11 legislation discussed in the Week 6 notes in connection with expanded U.S. surveillance powers. It belongs to the institutional setting in which counterterrorism justified more extensive information gathering.

The course uses this history to examine how security narratives normalize surveillance and reshape civil-liberty debates. The analytical focus is the combination of legal authority, intelligence institutions, and technical capacity.
