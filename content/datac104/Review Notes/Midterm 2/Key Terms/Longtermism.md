# Longtermism
Lectures: L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L20 AI Futures|L20: AI futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Longtermism assigns exceptional moral importance to humanity's distant future and potentially enormous future populations. L20 connects it to strands of effective altruism that shifted attention from present philanthropy toward existential risk.

The lecture asks how those calculations allocate attention and resources now. Even a small imagined change in extinction probability can dominate priorities when multiplied by a vast future population, raising questions about uncertainty and present needs.

---

## Examples

- AI appears as both a possible obstacle to the distant future and a tool for enabling digital minds, longevity, and space settlement.
