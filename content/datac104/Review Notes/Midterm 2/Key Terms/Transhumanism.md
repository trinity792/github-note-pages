# Transhumanism
Lectures: L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L20 AI Futures|L20: AI futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Transhumanism holds that humans can and should use science and technology to direct evolution and overcome biological limitations. The lecture traces aspirations for longer lives, enhanced intelligence, and space travel into later AI and singularity narratives.

The course examines these futures through mortality, meaning, and positionality. Technological self-transcendence can resemble secular redemption, while its costs, beneficiaries, and institutional control remain political questions.

---

## Examples

- 2001's transformation of Bowman into the Star Child dramatizes posthuman transcendence.
- Cryonics depends on hoped-for future capabilities to restore preserved people.
