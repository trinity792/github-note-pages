# OpenAI
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 9/Mona Sloane - The Business of AI|Sloane: the business of AI]].

## Definition and Significance
OpenAI is the AI organization used in L24 to examine frontier-model development and the pursuit of general-purpose machine intelligence. The lecture situates its models within strategies of scaling data, computation, and model size.

The analytical significance is the combination of projected capability and capital-intensive infrastructure. Investment expectations can advance more quickly than proven sources of profit, while the business depends on cloud services, chips, energy, expertise, and labor.

## Examples

- L24 connects Sam Altman's startup-network trajectory and Microsoft's investments to the resources required for scaling.
- Sloane contrasts speculative valuation with demonstrated revenue and productivity.
