# Risk Assessment
Lectures: L16, L17. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L16 Fairness Automated Decision-Making I|L16: fairness]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]].

## Definition and Significance
Risk assessment estimates the probability of a future event using recorded information and patterns. Automated systems translate such estimates into scores or categories that institutions use when deciding how to treat people.

The lectures emphasize the distinction between a prediction and the outcome produced through its use. Institutional targets, data, and responses can distribute liberty, resources, and surveillance while helping generate the future the model anticipates.

---

## Examples

- COMPAS supplies recidivism-risk classifications in criminal justice.
- AFST uses administrative records and proxies to inform child-welfare screening.
