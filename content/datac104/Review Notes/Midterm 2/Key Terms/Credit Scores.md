# Credit Scores
Lectures: L17, L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Credit scores convert selected financial records and past behavior into an impersonal assessment of repayment risk. L17 presents them as a way lenders decide whom to trust without relying on personal familiarity.

L18 emphasizes their role in producing class differences: access and prices affect future opportunities. Awareness of scoring also encourages people to manage both their conduct and the data through which institutions perceive them.

---

## Examples

- A low score can restrict borrowing or make credit more expensive, potentially reinforcing existing disadvantage.
