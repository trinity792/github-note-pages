# Disruptive Innovation
Lectures: L23. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L23 Making Silicon Valley II|L23: Silicon Valley II]].

## Definition and Significance
Disruptive innovation is the business-change model L23 associates with entry through cheaper, initially lower-quality products that attract users from established alternatives. New firms then improve their offerings over time.

The lecture also analyzes disruption as a Silicon Valley ideal. It can make experimentation and breaking existing arrangements appear desirable, directing attention toward growth while delaying questions about consequences or obligations.

---

## Examples

- A good-enough initial product can obtain users before it matches an incumbent's quality, creating a foothold for iterative development.
