# Platform Economy
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
The platform economy organizes economic exchanges through company-controlled digital infrastructures linking participants. It includes markets for goods, accommodation, transport, services, and labor.

L24 emphasizes that platform owners become powerful intermediaries. Network effects, investment, pricing, and control over participation reshape markets and employment, while activities previously outside recorded transactions become sources of revenue and data.

- Uber's expansion combined an app, subsidized prices, workers seeking income, and contested regulatory conditions.
