# Immortality and Longevity
Lectures: L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L20 AI Futures|L20: AI futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Longevity concerns extending life; immortality imagines escaping death indefinitely. L20 situates both within technological futures involving anti-aging practices, cryonics, mind uploading, and posthuman transformation.

The lecture complicates the promise by asking what remains human and who sustains prolonged existence. Apparently universal desires encounter unequal resources, infrastructure ownership, and the labor needed to maintain life.

---

## Examples

- The virtual-afterlife example in Black Mirror depends on servers, providers, and payment.
- Blade Runner connects demands for more life to imposed limits on exploited workers.
