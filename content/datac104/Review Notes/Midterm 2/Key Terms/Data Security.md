# Data Security
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]]; [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]].

## Definition and Significance
Data security concerns protecting and managing collected information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. It is one major part of the inherited privacy framework, alongside consent and enforcement.

Security and privacy overlap, but a securely operated system can still carry out an objectionable authorized use. The course therefore asks whether information should be collected or transferred at all, as well as how well it is protected.
