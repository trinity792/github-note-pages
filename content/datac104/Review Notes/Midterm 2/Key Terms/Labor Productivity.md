# Labor Productivity
Lectures: L21. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L21 Automation and Labor|L21: automation and labor]].

## Definition and Significance
Labor productivity measures output relative to labor input. Technical systems can increase it by enabling more output from the same work or reducing the work needed for a given output.

L21 separates productivity from its distribution: increased output does not guarantee higher wages or better working conditions. It also discusses the gap between enthusiasm for information technology and clearly demonstrated aggregate productivity gains.
