# Existential Risk
Lectures: L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L20 AI Futures|L20: AI futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Existential risk concerns threats to humanity's continued existence and possible future. L20 focuses on arguments that a radically capable AI might produce extinction through uncontrollable or misaligned pursuit of its objectives.

The lecture analyzes this discourse as a historically situated narrative rather than endorsing a particular forecast. Its moral urgency draws partly on the vast number of possible future lives that extinction would eliminate.

- The intelligence-explosion scenario imagines machine capabilities increasing beyond meaningful human control, even without hostility toward people.
