# Cybernetics and Cyborgs
Lectures: L15, L20. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]]; [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 8 – Data AI Futures|Week 8 futures review]].

## Definition and Significance
Cybernetics studies organization, information, feedback, and self-regulation across machines, organisms, and social systems. A cyborg is a hybrid figure crossing organism-machine boundaries; the notes use it to question a sharply isolated, purely biological human.

The concepts are related but distinct. L15 historicizes sciences that made purposive behavior mechanically describable, while the futures material contrasts technological fusion narratives with feminist accounts of hybridity and interdependence.

## Examples

- A thermostat exemplifies feedback: measured temperature changes subsequent heating or cooling.
- The Week 8 review uses Haraway's cyborg to challenge rigid human/machine and natural/artificial binaries.
