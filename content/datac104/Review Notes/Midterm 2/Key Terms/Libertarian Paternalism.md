# Libertarian Paternalism
Lectures: L15. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]].

## Definition and Significance
Libertarian paternalism is the combination underlying nudges: an authority steers people toward an outcome it regards as beneficial while preserving their formal ability to choose otherwise.

The course asks who defines the desirable result, establishes the options, and controls the environment. Retained choice therefore does not remove power; influence can operate through defaults and the structure of alternatives.

---

## Examples

- A parent offering two times for tooth brushing leaves a choice of timing while setting brushing itself as the expected outcome.
