# Move Fast and Break Things
Lectures: L23. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L23 Making Silicon Valley II|L23: Silicon Valley II]].

## Definition and Significance
Move fast and break things is Facebook's slogan used in L23 to exemplify startup culture. It celebrates speed, experimentation, and disruption of existing practices.

The course connects the slogan to performative founder optimism. It asks what follows when breaking arrangements becomes evidence of innovation and questions about rules or harms are postponed until after a firm grows powerful.
