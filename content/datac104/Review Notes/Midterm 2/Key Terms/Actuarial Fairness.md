# Actuarial Fairness
Lectures: L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Actuarial fairness is the principle that equal predicted risks should pay equal premiums and unequal risks should pay different premiums. Each person is expected to cover the loss attributed to their risk category.

The course emphasizes the political assumptions beneath this apparent precision. People are treated as equivalent to their group's average, while narrower classification can increase surveillance and undermine solidarity.

---

## Examples

- Gender-based insurance debates challenged one predictive category without necessarily rejecting individualized pricing.
- Higher predicted medical costs can make coverage more expensive for people already facing disadvantage.
