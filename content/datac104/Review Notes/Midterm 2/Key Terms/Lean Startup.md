# Lean Startup
Lectures: L23. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L23 Making Silicon Valley II|L23: Silicon Valley II]].

## Definition and Significance
The lean startup approach begins with a minimum viable product and uses short development cycles, user observation, experimentation, and pivots to develop a business. Metrics and growth can support subsequent financing.

L23 explains how this model reframes incomplete plans and uncertain products as disciplined learning. It fits software's capacity for iteration and a venture environment willing to fund projected scale before stable profit.

---

## Examples

- The operating sequence is to release an initial product, observe behavior, modify it, and use evidence of growth to seek another funding round.
