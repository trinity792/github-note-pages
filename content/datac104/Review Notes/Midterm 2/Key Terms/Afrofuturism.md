# Afrofuturism
Lectures: L19. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 8 – Data AI Futures|Week 8 futures review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L19 Data Futures|L19: data futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Afrofuturism combines speculative imagination, science fiction, history, and fantasy to envision Black technological agency, thriving, and liberation. The Week 8 review presents it as an alternative to exclusionary or Eurocentric technological futures.

The concept illustrates how positionality changes what improvement and flourishing can mean. Imagining from different experiences can reveal people and possibilities excluded by stories that present one version of humanity as universal.
