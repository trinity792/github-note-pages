# Fair Information Practices (FIPs or FIPPs)
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
Fair Information Practice Principles are the information-governance principles associated in the notes with the 1973 HEW report. They address notice, choice, access and participation, data integrity and security, and enforcement or redress.

L12 groups this inheritance into consent, security, and enforcement. These principles provide procedures for governing collected information, but the lecture argues that procedural compliance alone can overlook changed purposes, collective interests, dignity, and unequal power.
