# Futuring
Lectures: L19. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L19 Data Futures|L19: data futures]].

## Definition and Significance
Futuring comprises practices that make possible futures imaginable, tangible, or actionable. It includes stories, scenarios, design, planning, and prediction, with prediction only one part of the broader activity.

L19 emphasizes projection: imagined futures direct present attention, feelings, investment, and action. They also reveal assumptions about the narrator's current world and can begin enacting the arrangements they describe.

- Diana's smart environment makes legal consequences of anticipatory technology concrete.
- The Matrix organizes concern about hidden systems through dystopian fiction.
