# Cold War
Lectures: L15. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L15 Making Decisions|L15: making decisions]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
The Cold War is the postwar geopolitical setting through which the course explains military investment, strategic decision science, and electronics development. Nuclear confrontation intensified the stakes attributed to optimizing decisions and reducing human error.

The concept connects ideas about rational agency to institutions that funded and needed them. The later Silicon Valley history similarly locates technological capabilities within defense procurement and university-government relationships.
