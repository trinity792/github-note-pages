# Privacy Act (1974)
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
The Privacy Act of 1974 is the federal recordkeeping law discussed in the course as a response to fears about computerized government dossiers. It belongs to the shift toward treating privacy as individual control over personal information.

Its historical significance is the focus on government records within a broader, fragmented U.S. privacy regime. L12 asks whether that inherited framework can address later commercial data circulation, machine-learning uses, and collective harms.
