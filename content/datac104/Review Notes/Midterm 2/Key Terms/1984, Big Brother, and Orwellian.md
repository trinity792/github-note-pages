# 1984, Big Brother, and Orwellian
Lectures: L13, L19. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L19 Data Futures|L19: data futures]]; [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]].

## Definition and Significance
George Orwell's *1984* supplies a cultural template of centralized, totalitarian surveillance. Big Brother and the adjective Orwellian invoke an authority that watches people and demands subservience.

L19 asks what this template makes visible and what it hides. Its focus on an overpowering state can illuminate some surveillance arrangements while distracting from commercial platforms and distributed state-corporate networks.
