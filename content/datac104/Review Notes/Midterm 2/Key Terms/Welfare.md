# Welfare
Lectures: L17. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 7/Virginia Eubanks' Automating Inequality (Chapter 4)|Eubanks: the Allegheny algorithm]].

## Definition and Significance
Welfare refers here to institutional support intended to meet material needs and protect vulnerable people. L17 examines a means-tested system that gathers extensive information to determine eligibility and organize assistance.

The course highlights a contradiction when the same institution provides help and investigates recipients. Seeking support can produce records interpreted as evidence of risk, potentially discouraging eligible people from obtaining needed resources.
