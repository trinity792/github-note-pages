# Liberalism
Lectures: L14. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L17 Automated Decision Making II - Prediction|L17: prediction]].

## Definition and Significance
In this part of the course, liberalism concerns limiting arbitrary rule and governing through general, public, impersonal procedures. Quantification can appear aligned with that ideal because the same explicit rule applies across individuals.

The lectures distinguish formal uniformity from material equality. A bureaucracy can use consistent procedures while sorting people into unequal opportunities, so rule-following alone does not resolve questions about justice or the distribution of power.
