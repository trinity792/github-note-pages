# Santa Clara Valley
Lectures: L22. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]].

## Definition and Significance
Santa Clara Valley is the regional setting through which L22 examines Silicon Valley's layered development. The familiar story begins with orchards and agriculture before moving through electronics, semiconductors, and later computing.

The lecture challenges that starting point. Indigenous histories, colonization, agricultural labor, military demand, and institutional networks complicate a clean narrative of technological takeoff from an idyllic rural baseline.
