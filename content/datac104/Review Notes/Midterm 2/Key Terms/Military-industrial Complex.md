# Military-industrial Complex
Lectures: L22, L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L22 Making Silicon Valley I|L22: Silicon Valley I]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 9/Tianyu M Fang - Silicon Valley Goes To War|Fang: Silicon Valley goes to war]].

## Definition and Significance
The military-industrial complex names the interconnected military establishment, arms industry, and wider institutional networks discussed through Eisenhower's warning. The course locates electronics and Silicon Valley development within these relationships.

L24's defense pivot shows renewed private investment in capabilities purchased by states. The key questions concern public dependence, private influence, procurement, and the consequences of joining commercial incentives to national-security functions.

---

## Examples

- Early integrated circuits benefited from military purchasing.
- Palantir and SpaceX illustrate later private platforms supplying state capabilities.
