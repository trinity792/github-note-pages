# Technology as Capital
Lectures: L21. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L21 Automation and Labor|L21: automation and labor]]; [[datac104/Reading Notes/Week 8/David F. Noble - Social Choice in Machine Design The Case of Automatically Controlled Machine Tools|Noble: social choice in machine design]].

## Definition and Significance
Technology as capital means examining machinery as an owned productive asset within relations between employers and workers. Investment can substitute capital for labor and redistribute control over production.

The course asks how efficiency, authority, and accumulation interact. A design may be attractive because it weakens worker bargaining power or centralizes knowledge, even where simple cost-minimization does not explain its adoption.
