# Scaling Laws
Lectures: L24. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
Scaling laws are observed relationships connecting model performance with increases in quantities such as training data, computation, and model parameters. L24 describes these trends as a basis for pursuing larger AI systems.

The lecture treats the graphs as performative as well as descriptive. Projections help justify spending intended to continue the trajectory, while questions about profitability, resources, and ultimate capabilities remain open.

---

## Examples

- An improving-performance graph can attract capital for GPUs, energy, servers, and data centers needed to pursue further scale.
