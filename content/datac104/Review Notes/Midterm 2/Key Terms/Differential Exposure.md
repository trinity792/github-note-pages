# Differential Exposure
Lectures: L13. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
Differential exposure means that people are not equally subject to surveillance, scrutiny, or its consequences. Broad collection can coexist with concentrated monitoring and coercive effects for particular social groups.

The concept links privacy to identity, positionality, and power. A formally uniform technology may impose different burdens because its categories and institutional setting fit some people more readily than others.
