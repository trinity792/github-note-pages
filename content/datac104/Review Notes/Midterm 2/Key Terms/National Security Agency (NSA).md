# National Security Agency (NSA)
Lectures: L13. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 6 – Making Social Order|Week 6 social-order review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L24 Making Silicon Valley III|L24: Silicon Valley III]].

## Definition and Significance
The NSA is the U.S. intelligence agency at the center of the course's Snowden surveillance example. The notes connect it with large-scale collection and analysis of communications-related information.

Its significance is institutional as well as technical: surveillance involves secret programs, legal permissions, commercial infrastructure, and contracted work. L24 uses Snowden's contractor role to show how state functions can be carried out through private organizations.
