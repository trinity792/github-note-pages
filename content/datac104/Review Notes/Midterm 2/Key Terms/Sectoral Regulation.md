# Sectoral Regulation
Lectures: L11. Sources: [[datac104/Review Notes/Week 5 – Privacy|Week 5 privacy review]]; [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L12 Privacy in the World|L12: privacy in the world]].

## Definition and Significance
Sectoral regulation governs data through rules for particular fields or industries rather than one comprehensive privacy regime. The course presents the United States as a patchwork in which the relevant protection depends partly on the institution and kind of record.

This structure reflects the history of privacy governance. Data circulating across organizations and purposes can outgrow the categories used to protect it, especially when concerns involve group effects or contextual expectations rather than only identifiable records.
