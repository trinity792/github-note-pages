# Collectivizing and Individualizing Risk
Lectures: L18. Sources: [[datac104/Lecture Notes/L18 Automated Decision Making III - Risk|L18: risk]].

## Definition and Significance
Collectivizing risk shares uncertain losses across a population through mutual funds or social insurance. Individualizing risk assigns responsibility and cost more narrowly, often by pricing each person according to predicted outcomes.

The distinction is political: statistical regularities do not determine who should bear their costs. Different arrangements can make the same uncertainty a shared social obligation or a problem each individual must manage.

---

## Examples

- Workers' compensation pools resources for industrial injuries.
- Actuarial insurance separates customers into narrower groups with differentiated premiums.
