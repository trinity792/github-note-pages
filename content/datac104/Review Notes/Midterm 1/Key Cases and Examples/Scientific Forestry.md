## Scientific Forestry (_Normalbaum_) in Prussia
- **Source**: James C. Scott, _Seeing Like a State_ (Ch. 1) & _Lecture 8_
- **Summary**: In the late 18th century, Prussian state foresters replaced diverse, wild forests with standardized, single-species, row-planted spruce and pine forests (_Normalbaum_).
- **Additional Context**: Scott uses this to illustrate state **legibility** and the "view from above." The state simplified complex forest ecosystems into measurable units of timber revenue. While initially successful, this extreme abstraction caused ecological disaster within a generation (_Waldsterben_ / forest death), as single-species forests proved highly vulnerable to pests, storm damage, and soil exhaustion.
