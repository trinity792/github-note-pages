## Gapminder.org & Global Fertility Data
- **Source**: _Lecture 10_
- **Summary**: Hans Rosling’s Gapminder platform, which animates global income, life expectancy, and fertility data across nations over time.
- **Additional Context**: Demonstrates how post-WWII international organizations (UN, IMF) built a synoptic, global "view from above." Highlights how demographic data collection transitioned from Malthusian Cold War anxieties over "population explosions" in developing nations to modern frameworks centered on women's empowerment and economic metrics.
