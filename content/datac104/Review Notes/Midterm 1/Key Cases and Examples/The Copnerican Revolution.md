## The Copernican Revolution in Astronomy
- **Source**: Thomas Kuhn, _The Structure of Scientific Revolutions_ & _Lecture 6_
- **Summary**: The historic shift from Ptolemaic geocentric astronomy (which relied on epicycles) to Copernican heliocentric astronomy.
- **Additional Context**: Thomas Kuhn’s central case study for a **paradigm shift**. It illustrates how normal science operates within an accepted paradigm until persistent anomalies trigger a crisis, culminating in extraordinary science and a revolutionary "gestalt switch" or conversion experience across the scientific community.
