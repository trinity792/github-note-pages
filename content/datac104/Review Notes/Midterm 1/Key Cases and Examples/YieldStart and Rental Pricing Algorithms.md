## YieldStar & RealPage Rental Pricing Algorithms
- **Source**: _Lecture 2_ (citing _ProPublica_, _Wired_, _Inc._)
- **Summary**: RealPage’s YieldStar software collects non-public, real-time lease data from competing landlords to dynamically set rental prices for apartment units across the US.
- **Additional Context**: Demonstrates how datafication structures modern social order. The algorithm operates as an automated price-fixing mechanism, suppressing market competition and driving up housing costs. It illustrates a **sociotechnical system** where landlords, software algorithms, housing shortages, and DOJ antitrust regulations intersect in everyday life.
