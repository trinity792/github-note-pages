## Sierra Leone Healthcare vs. UK NHS & the "Medical Brain Drain"
- **Source**: Andrew Brooks, _Why Are Certain Countries Poor?_ (Ch. 8)
- **Summary**: A comparative analysis of healthcare outcomes at Connaught Hospital in Freetown, Sierra Leone, versus the British National Health Service (NHS).
- **Additional Context**: Brooks rejects linear development models (Rostow's stages) and Western volunteer narratives that frame Sierra Leonean hospitals as "going back in time." Using **relational comparison**, he shows that high Western health rankings are subsidized by the active extraction of medical labor from the Global South: 60% of Sierra Leonean-trained nurses emigrate, and 16% of Sierra Leone's public health workforce works in the UK NHS.
