## The L'Aquila Earthquake Trial
- **Source**: _Lecture 7_
- **Summary**: In 2012, an Italian court convicted seven scientists and government officials of manslaughter following a 2009 earthquake in L'Aquila, Italy, that killed over 300 people.
- **Additional Context**: Illustrates the crisis of **public trust, risk communication, and expertise**. Prior to the tremor, officials had given overly reassuring public statements to prevent panic. The trial highlighted the danger of projecting absolute certainty in the face of inherently uncertain scientific phenomena.
