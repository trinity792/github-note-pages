## Eugenic Statistics (Galton, Pearson, Fisher, and Terman)
- **Source**: Audrey Clayton / Kristen French, _"How Eugenics Shaped Statistics"_ (Nautilus) & _Lecture 9_
- **Summary**: Victorian and early 20th-century scientists—Francis Galton, Karl Pearson, Ronald Fisher, and Lewis Terman—developed core statistical tools while advancing eugenics.
- **Additional Context**: Proves that modern mathematical statistics was co-produced with eugenics:
    - **Francis Galton**: Invented linear regression and correlation specifically to measure the inherited talent of elite families.
    - **Karl Pearson**: Created the chi-squared test to measure "homogeneity," arguing for racial purity and opposing Jewish immigration into the UK.
    - **Ronald Fisher**: Developed ANOVA and p-value significance testing while lobbying for state-mandated sterilization of the "feeble-minded."
    - **Lewis Terman**: Adapted the Binet IQ test at Stanford to rank intelligence, justify racial hierarchies, and influence US immigration restrictions.
