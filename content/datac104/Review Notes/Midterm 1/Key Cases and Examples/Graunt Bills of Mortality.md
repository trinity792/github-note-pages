## John Graunt’s Bills of Mortality
- **Source**: _Lecture 8_
- **Summary**: 17th-century London mortality tallies compiled by John Graunt (_Political Arithmetick_) and 19th-century work by Belgian astronomer Adolphe Quetelet (_l'homme moyen_).
- **Additional Context**: Marks the birth of modern demography, statistics, and **biopower**. Graunt showed that aggregated mortality data revealed stable social patterns. Quetelet adapted the astronomical normal curve to human physical and moral traits, conceptualizing the mathematical "average" as nature's ideal type.
