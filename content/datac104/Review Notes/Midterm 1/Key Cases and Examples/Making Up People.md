## MPD "Making Up People"
- **Source**: Ian Hacking, _"Making Up People"_ & _Lecture 5_
- **Summary**: The rapid surge in diagnoses of Multiple Personality Disorder (now Dissociative Identity Disorder) during the 1980s following its inclusion in the DSM-III.
- **Additional Context**: Used by philosopher Ian Hacking to explain **performativity** and the **"looping effect."** When medical experts create a new category, institutions (clinics, talk shows, insurance forms) and knowledge networks disseminate it, creating a "moving target." Individuals adopt the classification to make sense of their suffering, changing how they act and ultimately altering the category itself.
