This system created an agonizing **epistemological conflict between "Aristotelian" sorting** (the state's legal attempt to enforce mutually exclusive, pure categories) **and "prototypical" sorting** (the messy, on-the-spot visual judgments and social reputes that actually governed daily life). When these rigid, artificial legal categories were forced onto the complex lived realities of individuals, it produced **severe biographical "torque"—a twisting friction that disrupted human lives, tore families apart, and shattered careers**.
