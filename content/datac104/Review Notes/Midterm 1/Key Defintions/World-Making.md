## World-Making
- **Definition**: The capacity of technical systems, data, and classifications to actively construct new social orders, legal regimes, economic arrangements, and cultural meanings rather than merely describing an existing reality (`Lecture 2, slide 7`).
- **Differentiating Contexts**:
    - _World-Making:_ Focuses broadly on how data infrastructures build macro-level social order and institutional realities (`Lecture 2, slide 7`).
    - _Performativity (Hacking / Butler):_ Specifically focuses on how classifications and descriptions create new ways of being a human self or social identity (`Hacking p. 65–66`; `Lecture 5, slides 3–5`).
