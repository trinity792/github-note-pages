## Underdetermination
- **Definition**: The philosophical principle that empirical data alone underdetermines scientific theories—meaning that data is insufficient to uniquely prove a single theory, and alternative, logically incompatible theories can always be constructed to fit the same observations (`Lecture 6, slide 7`; `Kuhn p. 146`).
