## Expertise
- **Definition & Differentiations**:
    - **Formal Scientific Expertise**: Credentialed, institutionalized knowledge based on abstract models, standardized experiments, and laboratory conditions (`Collins & Pinch p. 117`).
    - **Lay / Practical Expertise**: Localized, experiential craft knowledge possessed by non-credentialed practitioners (e.g., Cumbrian sheep farmers) based on long-term observation of local environments and practices (`Collins & Pinch p. 117–120`).
