## Naturalize / Denaturalize
- **Definitions**:
    - **Naturalize**: The social process by which human choices, classification categories, and technical standards become so deeply embedded and routine that they appear neutral, natural, and self-evident (`Lecture 3, slide 7`; `Bowker & Star p. 199`).
    - **Denaturalize**: The critical practice of unpacking data representations, algorithms, and technical systems to expose the hidden human judgments, political interests, and historical choices behind them (`Lecture 3, slide 7`).
