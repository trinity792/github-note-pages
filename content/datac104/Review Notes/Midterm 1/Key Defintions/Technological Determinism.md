## Technological Determinism
- **Definition**: The reductive belief that technology develops independently according to its own internal logic and directly dictates social, political, and economic outcomes (`Lecture 2, slide 5`).
- **Differentiating Contexts**:
    - _Technological Determinism:_ Assumes technology is an autonomous cause that shapes passive human societies (`Lecture 2, slide 5`).
    - _Sociotechnical Framework (Winner / HCE):_ Rejects determinism, arguing that artifacts and technical systems are inherently political, built out of human choices, and intertwined with social power and institutional structures (`Winner p. 121–122`; `Lecture 2, slide 5`).
