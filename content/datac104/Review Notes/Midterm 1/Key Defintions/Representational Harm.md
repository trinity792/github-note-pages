## Representational Harm
- **Definition & Differentiation**:
    - **Representational Harm**: Harm that occurs when a system, algorithm, or data categorization misrepresents, degrades, or stigmatizes a group or individual (e.g., Google Photos auto-tagging Black people as "gorillas" or offensive demographic labels), regardless of whether material resources are withheld (`Lecture 4, slide 6`).
    - _Allocative Harm:_ Contrastingly, allocative harm involves the unfair or discriminatory distribution of concrete opportunities or resources (e.g., denial of housing, loans, or bail) (`Lecture 4, slide 6`).
