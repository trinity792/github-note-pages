## Abstraction
- **Definition**: The process of decontextualizing complex real-world phenomena, places, or individuals by converting them into standardized, simplified units or data points (`Lecture 3, slide 4`; `Scott p. 11`).
- **Differentiating Contexts**:
    - _In Data Creation (Lecture 3 & 4):_ Stripping away local context so data can circulate across different systems (`Lecture 4, slide 5`).
    - _In Statecraft (James Scott):_ The state's reduction of old-growth forests into geometric tree metrics (_Normalbaum_) or complex communal land tenure into flat cadastral maps (`Scott p. 11–15`).
    - _In Educational Computing (Lubar / Free Speech Movement):_ The reduction of students to two-dimensional IBM punch cards (`Lecture 4, slide 2`).
