## Cyborgs
- **Definition**: A concept (derived from Donna Haraway and Colin Koopman) arguing that human identity and personhood are not purely isolated biological selves, but are fundamentally extended into and constituted by material devices, digital networks, and data trails ("we are cyborgs who extend into our data") (`Lecture 5, slide 5`; Koopman quoted in `Lecture 5, slide 5`).
