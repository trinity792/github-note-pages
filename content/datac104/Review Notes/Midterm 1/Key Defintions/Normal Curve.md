## Normal Curve
- **Differentiating Historical Interpretations**:
    - _Quetelet's "Average Man":_ Adolphe Quetelet adapted the astronomical error curve to human traits, viewing the population average as nature's ideal type and deviations as accidental errors (`Lecture 8, slide 6`; `Lecture 9, slide 2`).
    - _Galton's Eugenic Curve:_ Francis Galton reinterpreted the bell curve into a hierarchical tool of "genetic worth," ranking desirable elite traits above "undesired" deviations to justify eugenic intervention (`Lecture 9, slide 2`; `French / Nautilus p. 63`).
