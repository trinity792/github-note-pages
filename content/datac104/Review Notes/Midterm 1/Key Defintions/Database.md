## Database
- **Definition**: An organized, persistent collection of digital records that allows personal and administrative data to be stored, queried, cross-referenced, and circulated across institutional domains (`Lecture 4, slide 8`).
- **Case Examples**: The Allegheny County DHS data warehouse used for predictive child welfare screening (`Eubanks p. 120`) or the central reference book database used for race classification under South African Apartheid (`Bowker & Star p. 200`)
