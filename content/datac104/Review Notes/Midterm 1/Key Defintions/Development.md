## Development
- **Definition & Differentiating Frameworks**:
    - _Mainstream Linear Model (Rostow / UN):_ Depicts development as a universal, step-by-step modernization ladder where poor nations are simply "behind" affluent Western nations (`Brooks p. 82–85`).
    - _Relational Comparison (Andrew Brooks):_ Critiques linear development models as developmental fantasies, showing that Western core wealth is historically and structurally codependent on the active underdevelopment and exploitation of the Global South (`Brooks p. 82–94`).
