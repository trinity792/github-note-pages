## Co-Production
Technology and society are mutually constitutive, or, in other words, they depend on one another for the forms they assume: technology makes society what it is, and society makes technology what it is. Or, “Technology is co-produced with society.”

**Exemplary cases:**
- Ideas of privacy co-produced with social media platforms
- Statistical tools are co-produced with the formation of the  
    nation-state
- Ethical codes are co-produced with professions
- Labor conditions in the datafied world are co-produced with technologies of surveillance

**Questions to ask with this tool:**
- What technology is co-produced with what aspect of society?
- How does this co-production take place?
- What’s at stake in these two things being co-produced?
