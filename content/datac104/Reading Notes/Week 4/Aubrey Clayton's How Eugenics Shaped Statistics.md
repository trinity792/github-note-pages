# How Eugenics Shaped Statistics - Exposing the Entangled History of Science and Prejudice

> [!flag] Reading Question
> How should we think about statistics, given the history described here? Assuming we aren’t going to stop using statistics, what does this information help us to do?

## Executive Summary

In "How Eugenics Shaped Statistics," mathematician Aubrey Clayton argues that the modern discipline of statistics did not emerge as a neutral, value-free mathematical science but was explicitly forged as an empirical tool to validate and advance the early 20th-century eugenics movement. The primary pioneers of statistical methodology—Francis Galton, Karl Pearson, and Ronald Fisher—developed foundational concepts such as correlation, regression, the chi-squared test, and the analysis of variance (ANOVA) specifically to quantify human difference, justify racial hierarchies, and advocate for state-mandated social engineering, such as selective breeding and forced sterilization. This historical entanglement reveals that many of the theoretical flaws and limitations of modern statistics are direct remnants of their original eugenicist purposes.

The central pillar of this scientific project was the concept of "statistical significance" and significance testing. Originally designed by Karl Pearson and popularized by Ronald Fisher, the method of rejecting the "null hypothesis" served primarily taxonomic and discriminatory ends. By focusing on a binary "question of whether" a difference existed rather than "how much" that difference mattered—what critics Deirdre McCloskey and Stephen Ziliak call the "sizeless stare" of significance—eugenicists were able to mathematically reify racial separations, categorize marginalized immigrant populations as "inferior," and shield their social prejudices behind an unassailable veneer of objective, unassailable number-crunching.

This historical legacy is undergoing a double reckoning today, both culturally and methodologically. Culturally, institutions like University College London and major scientific societies are removing the names of Galton, Pearson, and Fisher from classrooms and prizes to confront their white supremacist pasts. Methodologically, the dominance of significance testing is crumbling as modern scientists acknowledge its systemic flaws—exemplified by David Lykken and Paul Meehl’s "crud factor" which demonstrates that sufficiently large datasets will yield "statistically significant" correlations for almost any arbitrary pairing of variables. Clayton concludes that to truly decolonize and rehabilitate statistics, the discipline must dismantle the myth of perfect mathematical objectivity, address its severe underrepresentation of marginalized groups, and adopt a moral commitment to justice that acknowledges the human stakes of numerical authority.

## Key Arguments and Evidence
In his article **"How Eugenics Shaped Statistics: Exposing the Damned Lies of Three Science Pioneers,"** mathematician and author **Aubrey Clayton** presents a rigorous critique of the historical and conceptual foundations of modern statistical methods.
### Primary Argument
Clayton’s central argument is that **statistical thinking and eugenicist thinking are deeply and structurally intertwined**. Modern statistics was not developed as a neutral, value-free mathematical toolkit that was later co-opted by bad actors; rather, foundational techniques—including correlation, regression, chi-squared tests, \(p\)-values, and null-hypothesis significance testing (NHST)—were **explicitly engineered by Francis Galton, Karl Pearson, and Ronald Fisher to quantify racial differences, prove genetic hierarchies, and justify eugenic social policies**.

Furthermore, Clayton argues that these three pioneers deliberately constructed a **"myth of objectivity"** and an image of the statistician as an impartial "numerical referee" to shield their racist and elitist social agendas behind a veneer of unassailable mathematical authority. Consequently, modern efforts to rename awards or remove eugenicists' names from university buildings are merely surface-level symbolic actions; true reform requires a fundamental **reckoning with the discipline's origins, abandoning the illusion of perfect objectivity, and democratizing the data analysis process**.

> [!quote] 
> "Statistical thinking and eugenicist thinking are, in fact, deeply intertwined, and many of the theoretical problems with methods like significance testing—first developed to identify racial differences—are remnants of their original purpose, to support eugenics."


> [!quote]
> "Crumbling alongside them is the image of statistics as a perfectly objective discipline, another legacy of the three eugenicists... The buildings and lectures are the monuments to eugenics we can see. The less visible ones are embedded in the language, logic, and philosophy of statistics itself."
### Key Terms and Definitions Used by Clayton

- **Eugenics**: Coined by Francis Galton from the Greek for "well born," defined by Karl Pearson as **"the directed and self-conscious evolution of the human race"**.
- **Normal Distribution / Bell Curve**: Originally used in astronomy and geodesy to model measurement errors, and by Adolphe Quetelet to conceptualize the "average man". Galton reinterpreted the bell curve to construct separate distribution scales for distinct racial groups to measure "how substantial the racial differences were".
- **Regression and Correlation**: Mathematical techniques created by Galton to measure how strongly one variable predicts another, specifically designed to calculate the heritability of human abilities across generations.
- **Null Hypothesis Significance Testing (NHST) and \(p\)-value**: A three-part analytical template formulated by Pearson (via the chi-squared \(\chi^2\) test) and expanded by Fisher [18–20, 43]. It sets up a "null hypothesis" straw-man (e.g., that a population is homogeneous or of one species) and uses a test statistic and ($p$)-value threshold (\(p < 0.05\)) to measure whether deviations are "statistically significant".
- **Homogeneity**: A statistical term used by Pearson to denote uniform data, which was explicitly tied to his eugenic requirement for national racial purity: 
> [!quote]
> "Around the same time that Pearson was describing his method for recognizing non-normal data by writing, ‘The asymmetry may arise from the fact that the units grouped together in the measured material are not really homogeneous,’ he was also describing his theory of nations by saying, ‘The nation organized for the struggle must be a homogeneous whole, not a mixture of superior and inferior races.’".
- **The "Sizeless Stare" of Statistical Significance**: A concept borrowed from economists Deirdre McCloskey and Stephen Ziliak, noting that significance testing asks a qualitative, binary question about existence ("whether" an effect exists) rather than a scientific question ("how much" or "why it matters"). Clayton notes this served eugenicists who only needed to claim that a statistical difference between groups existed to justify discrimination.
### Supporting Evidence, Cases, and Justifications
Clayton supports his argument by tracing a chronological line through the three foundational figures of modern statistics, supported by historical studies and empirical critiques:
#### 1. Francis Galton: Applying Evolution to Human "Breeding"
- **Case & Evidence**: In his 1869 book _Hereditary Genius_, Galton calculated family ties among prominent individuals to argue that genius was inherited, applying his half-cousin Charles Darwin's evolutionary theory to humans. In his chapter "The Comparative Worth of Different Races," Galton claimed that **"the average intellectual standard of the Negro race is some two grades below our own,"** attributing this entirely to heredity. In a letter to _The Times_, Galton advocated that the coast of Africa be handed over to Chinese colonists so they might **"supplant the inferior Negro race"**, referring to Africans as **"lazy, palavering savages"**.
- **Justification**: Galton adapted Quetelet’s bell curve specifically to construct a comparative ranking of human races, giving Victorian colonialism and white supremacy the appearance of scientific backing.
#### 2. Karl Pearson: Mathematical Rigor in Service of Imperialist Warfare
- **Case & Evidence**: Pearson created the chi-squared ($(\chi^2)$) test and founded _Biometrika_ in 1901. One of his earliest examples applied the test to skull measurements from 5th–7th century Reihengräber graves in Germany, arguing that statistical asymmetry **"signified the presence of two races of people"**.
- **1904 Tetrachoric Correlation Study**: Pearson analyzed 4,000 pairs of siblings, finding similar correlation values between physical traits (eye color) and personality traits ("vivacity," "assertiveness"). He concluded that personality traits were entirely inherited, asserting: **"You must breed it, that is the broad result for statecraft which flows from the equality in inheritance of the psychical and the physical characters in man."**.
- **1925 Anti-Semitic Immigrant Study**: Writing in the _Annals of Eugenics_, Pearson published a study on Jewish immigrant children fleeing eastern European pogroms, claiming they were less intelligent than native children and that environment had no impact, concluding: **"into a crowded country only the superior stocks should be allowed entrance..."** [24–26].
- **The "Objective" Mask**: To insulate his eugenic conclusions, Pearson insisted on his absolute neutrality: **"We have no axes to grind... We firmly believe that we have no political, no religious and no social prejudices... We rejoice in numbers and figures for their own sake..."**.
#### 3. Ronald Fisher: Significance Testing and Ties to Nazi Eugenics
- **Case & Evidence**: Fisher introduced significance testing, ANOVA, maximum likelihood, and the \(F\)-test in his landmark 1925 textbook _Statistical Methods for Research Workers_. Yet, the final third of his major biological text, _The Genetical Theory of Natural Selection_ (1930), was an explicit eugenic manifesto warning that higher fertility among lower social classes would cause the collapse of civilization.
- **Sterilization & Collaboration with Nazi Scientists**: Fisher co-formed the Committee for Legalizing Eugenic Sterilization in 1930, cited data from American eugenicists, and corresponded with Nazi eugenicist Ernst Rüdin. After WWII, Fisher publicly defended Otmar Freiherr von Verschuer (mentor to Josef Mengele), writing: **"I have no doubt also that the [Nazi] Party sincerely wished to benefit the German racial stock, especially by the elimination of manifest defectives... and I do not doubt that von Verschuer gave, as I should have done, his support to such a movement."**. In 1951, Fisher dissented against UNESCO's anti-racist statement "The Race Question," insisting human groups differed in innate intellectual capacity.
#### 4. Empirical Proof of Statistical Flaws: The Meehl & Lykken 1968 Study
- **Case & Evidence**: To prove that significance testing is logically flawed and yields false confirmations, Clayton highlights an empirical study by psychologists David Lykken and Paul Meehl. Analyzing 57,000 questionnaires from Minnesota high school students across 105 cross-tabulated variables, they found that **every single association was statistically significant, and 96% had \(p\)-values less than 0.000001** (e.g., birth order was significantly linked to interest in cooking or farm youth club membership).
- **Justification**: Meehl used this to prove that in large real-world datasets, subtle background noise always creates statistically significant differences, concluding that: **"Sir Ronald [Fisher] has befuddled us, mesmerized us, and led us down the primrose path..."**.
#### 5. Decolonizing Statistics & The Path Forward
- **Case & Evidence**: Clayton cites healthcare data scientist Nathaniel Joselson ("Meditations on Inclusive Statistics"), who notes that **"Objectivity is extremely overrated... What the future of science needs is a democratization of the analysis process..."**. Clayton also quotes Stanislav Andreski's _Social Sciences as Sorcery_ (1972), which warned that researchers use statistical methods as **"quantitative camouflage"**.
- **Conclusion**: Clayton concludes that scientists must reject the myth of pure objectivity and recognize that statistics is a human enterprise subject to social values: _"We cannot escape the fact that statistics is a human enterprise subject to human desire, prejudice, consensus, and interpretation."_.

## The Eugenicist Pioneers and the Quantification of Heredity

### Francis Galton: Coining Eugenics and Mapping the Normal Curve

- **The Father of Eugenics**: Francis Galton, a well-born Victorian gentleman and half-cousin to Charles Darwin, coined the term "eugenics" (from the Greek for "well born") to describe his vision of state-directed human breeding. Driven by Darwin's theory of evolution, Galton argued that human intelligence, talent, and social success were governed entirely by nature rather than nurture (a phrase he also coined), meaning the state should actively encourage elite breeding while discouraging reproduction among the "feeble-minded," criminals, and paupers to build a "galaxy of genius".
- **Scientific Racism and the Normal Curve**: In his 1869 book _Hereditary Genius_, Galton attempted to mathematically prove that natural ability runs in families. To support his white supremacist view that the average intellect of Black people was "two grades below" that of white Europeans, Galton adapted the normal distribution (bell curve). Originally used by astronomers to map measurement errors and by Adolphe Quetelet to conceptualize the "average man," Galton insisted that the curve applied strictly to "men of the same race". By plotting separate bell curves for white Europeans, Africans, and Asians, Galton sought to provide scientific, mathematical backing for British colonialism and racial violence.
- **The Invention of Correlation and Regression**: To quantify the strength of these inherited traits across generations, Galton invented the fundamental statistical tools of regression and correlation. These mathematical operations, designed to measure the degree to which one variable predicts another, were constructed specifically as tools to track the transmission of physical and mental qualities down family lineages. Toward the end of his life, Galton declared a "Jehad" or Holy War to mobilize public opinion and state power in service of these eugenicist truths.

### Karl Pearson: The Mathematics of Racial Purity and Genocide

- **Eugenics as Directed Evolution**: Karl Pearson, recognized as the creator of mathematical statistics, was a prolific Cambridge graduate who viewed eugenics as the "directed and self-conscious evolution of the human race". Pearson held extreme social-Darwinist and racist beliefs, arguing in his 1900 address "National Life from the Standpoint of Science" that nations must be kept internally efficient by breeding "better stocks" and externally efficient by engaging in "war with inferior races" to weed out bad stock.
- **Praising Colonial Genocide**: Pearson defended colonial violence and genocide as necessary instruments of progress. He famously praised the slaughter and displacement of Native Americans (the "Red man") in _The Grammar of Science_, asserting that it was a "true humanism" to replace a "dark-skinned tribe" that could not utilize its land with a "stalwart race of white men" who could contribute to the common stock of human knowledge.
- **The Biometrika Infrastructure and Xenophobia**: In 1901, Pearson co-founded _Biometrika_, which served as the premiere journal for statistical theory and a platform to broadcast his eugenicist findings. In 1925, Pearson utilized his journal _Annals of Eugenics_ to argue against Jewish immigration into the United Kingdom, publishing a study of Jewish children that used complex "tetrachoric correlations" to "prove" that immigrant children were on average less intelligent than native children and that their intelligence was unaffected by environmental improvements like health or nutrition.

### Ronald Fisher: Reconciling Darwin, Mendel, and Sterilization

- **Eugenics-Flavored Evolutionary Biology**: Ronald Fisher, widely celebrated for reconciling Mendelian genetics with Darwinian evolution in his 1930 book _The Genetical Theory of Natural Selection_, was a passionate eugenicist from his undergraduate days at Cambridge. The final third of his seminal biological text is a eugenicist manifesto warning that high fertility rates among the lower classes would bring down the British Empire. To combat this, Fisher proposed targeted financial disincentives to limit the family sizes of low-social-status citizens and immigrants.
- **Lobbying for Sterilization and Collaborating with Nazis**: Fisher served as the student chair of the Cambridge Eugenics Society and went on to write ninety-one articles for _The Eugenics Review_. In 1930, Fisher joined the British Eugenics Society's Committee for Legalizing Eugenic Sterilization, contributing statistical analyses to promote the sterilization of "feeble minded high-grade defectives". To support this campaign, the committee collaborated directly with Nazi eugenicist Ernst Rüdin, who expressed mutual admiration for Fisher's work.
- **Defending Nazi Scientists and Opposing UNESCO**: Following World War II, Fisher used his academic prestige to defend Nazi geneticist Otmar Freiherr von Verschuer (a mentor to concentration camp doctor Josef Mengele), writing that the Nazi party had "sincerely wished to benefit the German racial stock". In 1950, when UNESCO issued "The Race Question" to condemn racism on scientific grounds, Fisher wrote a formal dissent, asserting that human groups differ profoundly in their "innate capacity for intellectual and emotional development".

## The Mechanics and Ideology of Significance Testing

### Pearson’s Chi-Squared Test and the Homogeneity Obsession

- **The Chi-Squared (Χ2) Formula**: One of the first theoretical problems Pearson solved was determining whether empirical observations fit a theoretical normal distribution. Inspired by Weldon's unusual measurements of crab shells, Pearson developed the chi-squared test to calculate the mathematical "distance" between actual data and a theoretical distribution. High values of \(X^2\) indicated that the deviations were too large to occur purely by chance, signaling that the dataset was not "homogeneous".
- **The Racial Undertones of Homogeneity**: Pearson’s obsession with statistical "homogeneity" in data was deeply tied to his eugenicist obsession with racial homogeneity in nation-building. Just as non-normal data signified a mixture of different species or groups, Pearson argued that a "nation organized for the struggle must be a homogeneous whole, not a mixture of superior and inferior races". He applied this test to skull measurements from graves of the Reihengräber culture to argue that skull asymmetry "signified" the co-existence of two distinct races, rendering statistical asymmetry axiomatic to eugenicist classifications of character and intelligence.

### The Core Template of Significance Testing

- **The Straw-Man Null Hypothesis**: Pearson's work established the standard three-part template of modern significance testing:
    1. **Hypothesize a distribution**: Propose a "null hypothesis" that assumes homogeneity (e.g., "all individuals belong to the same species/race").
    2. **Compute the test statistic**: Use a tool like Pearson's \(X^2\) to measure how far the actual data deviates from this assumption.
    3. **Calculate the p-value**: Decide whether to reject the null hypothesis based on the probability (p-value) of getting a deviation that large by chance.
- **The Origin of "Significance"**: In Pearson's era, the word "significant" did not mean "important" or "large" in magnitude. It was simply the adjective form of "signify" (i.e., "indicative of"), meaning that the experimental data was indicative of the truth or falsehood of a specific hypothesis to a certain degree of mathematical probability.
- **The Fisherian Standardization**: Ronald Fisher standardized significance testing in his 1925 textbook _Statistical Methods for Research Workers_. Fisher promoted these "recipes" as the universal logic of all experimentation, establishing a p-value of less than 5 percent as the rigid, binary cutoff for scientific publication.

### The Illusion of Objective Impartiality

- **The "Unbiased Number-Cruncher" Myth**: To protect their highly controversial social engineering proposals from public backlash, Galton, Pearson, and Fisher established the myth of the perfectly objective statistician. They argued that the statistician was merely an impartial numerical referee, and that statistical formulas revealed fundamental, unquestionable truths as certain as gravity.
- **The Rhetoric of "Data Alone"**: Pearson vehemently denied having any political, religious, or social prejudices, claiming his lab was "paid by nobody to reach results of a given bias". Fisher similarly asserted that "conclusions can be drawn from data alone" without prior knowledge. By slathering their eugenicist arguments in hundreds of pages of complex formulas and technical jargon, they successfully intimidated critics, forcing anyone who wished to dispute their racist conclusions to first wade through an unassailable barrier of mathematical proof.

## The Modern Methodological and Cultural Reckoning

### The "Sizeless Stare" and the "Crud Factor"

- **The Problem of "Whether" vs. "How Much"**: Economists Deirdre McCloskey and Stephen Ziliak critique significance testing as a qualitative, philosophical test rather than a scientific one. By focusing entirely on whether an effect exists (a binary reject/accept decision), significance testing ignores the critical scientific questions of "how much" an effect matters, its causal mechanism, and its practical utility.
- **The Meehl and Lykken "Crud Factor" Demonstration**: In 1968, psychologists David Lykken and Paul Meehl analyzed 57,000 questionnaires from Minnesota high school students. Out of 105 possible cross-tabulations of completely unrelated variables (such as birth order, interest in cooking, religious views, and membership in farm youth clubs), every single association was found to be statistically significant, and 96 percent had p-values less than 0.000001.
- **The Fallacy of the Null Hypothesis**: Meehl argued that in the real world, everything is correlated with everything else to some degree (the "crud factor"). Therefore, with a large enough sample size (\(N\)), a researcher can reject the null hypothesis and "refute" a straw man for almost any arbitrary pairing of variables. Meehl concluded that Sir Ronald Fisher had "befuddled us, mesmerized us, and led us down the primrose path," calling the reliance on significance testing "one of the worst things that ever happened in the history of psychology".

### Decolonizing Statistics and Rejecting False Objectivity

- **Dismantling the Monuments**: Today, the historical prestige of eugenics is collapsing alongside its statistical methods. Major universities and statistical societies are actively removing the names of Galton, Pearson, and Fisher from buildings and prizes, with the American Statistical Association issuing a blunt decree: "Don’t say ‘statistically significant’".
- **Decolonizing the Lens**: Healthcare data scientist Nathaniel Joselson argues that statistics is long overdue for a "decolonization" to address its eugenicist legacy, which continues to cause systemic harm in criminal justice and education. Joselson asserts that "objectivity is extremely overrated" and calls for a democratization of the analysis process, reminding scientists that "just because you haven’t measured something doesn’t mean that it’s not there".
- **Addressing Systemic Exclusion**: Modern biostatisticians, including Mount Sinai professor Emma Benn, point out that treating the history of eugenics as a dry, purely intellectual exercise ignores the lived experience of marginalized scholars. Surveys show that Black, Latinx, and Indigenous students remain severely underrepresented in statistics, a direct consequence of a discipline designed by elite gatekeepers who explicitly structured the academy to "exclude all inferior types" and protect their own demographic affluence.

## Exploration via the Human Contexts and Ethics (HCE) Toolkit

### Power

- **Epistemic Authority and Academic Gatekeeping**: Power manifests as the asymmetric capacity of Galton, Pearson, and Fisher to define the scientific rules of validation. By establishing their own methods as the "gold standard" for publication, they acted as academic gatekeepers, determining which research was deemed credible and which voices were systematically excluded.
- **Weaponizing Mathematical Complexity**: Pearson and Fisher used the sheer complexity of mathematical statistics to intimidate critics and insulate their eugenicist political agendas from democratic accountability, forcing lay opponents to capitulate to the "unbiased" authority of the numerical referee.

### Sociotechnical Systems

- **Statistics as a Co-constitutive Lens**: Statistics acts as a powerful, non-neutral sociotechnical lens that shapes how we observe and structure society. The mathematical formulas (p-values, chi-squared calculations) cannot be isolated from the material institutions—such as University College London, the Galton Laboratory, the Cold Spring Harbor Eugenics Record Office, and state sterilization boards—that deployed them to legally commit "feeble-minded" citizens to state colonies.
- **Quantitative Camouflage**: In his 1972 book _Social Sciences as Sorcery_, Stanislav Andreski coined the term "quantitative camouflage" to describe how researchers use statistical jargon as an intellectual shield. This camouflage integrates mathematical tools with academic publishing systems to hide social and political biases under a mask of objective science.

### Identity/Positionality

- **The Elite Standpoint of the Pioneers**: The positionality of Galton, Pearson, and Fisher as elite, white British males operating at the height of European colonialism deeply shaped their scientific output. When they imagined a eugenicist utopia, they pictured a homogeneous society composed exclusively of individuals like themselves, using their class privilege to rationalize colonial violence and exclude marginalized "others".
- **Lived Experience vs. Esoteric Math**: Emma Benn’s critique highlights how the professional identity of modern statisticians of color is impacted by these historical legacies. The systemic exclusion and underrepresentation of Black and Hispanic scholars in the field today is a direct structural echo of a discipline built on eugenicist axioms of human worth and biological determinism.

### Narratives

- **The Narrative of Perfect Objectivity**: The pioneers of statistics constructed a powerful narrative of "unbiased number-crunching" to depict their findings as objective, natural laws on par with gravity. This narrative served to distance the scientists from the real-life human suffering caused by their policies, such as forced sterilization and xenophobic immigration bans.
- **The Counter-Narrative of Decolonization and Moral Objectivity**: Modern critics are constructing a powerful counter-narrative of "inclusive statistics". This narrative rejects the impossible standard of value-free objectivity, replacing it with what Stanislav Andreski calls "moral objectivity"—a transparent commitment to fairness, justice, and the courage to resist the temptations of wishful or venomous thinking.
